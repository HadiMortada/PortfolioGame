Experiment controls

Exact policy Jev must apply
  1. Choose the incident with the highest severity.
  2. If tied, choose the one with the shortest deadline.
  3. If still tied, choose the one affecting the most users.
  4. If still tied, choose the smallest ticket number.
The browser independently computes the correct answer from the structured fields. It sends Jev only the policy and human-readable candidate descriptions — never the oracle answer.
